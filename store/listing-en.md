## Target audience

Choose **13 and over** (or 13–15, 16–17, 18+). Choosing an under-13 group puts the app under the Google Play Families policy, which restricts which ad networks you may use.

## Data safety form: suggested answers

The game itself stores progress only on the device. The AdMob SDK collects the following:

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device or other IDs (Advertising ID) | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention |
| Approximate location (derived from IP) | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing |
| App interactions | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing, Analytics |
| Crash logs / diagnostics | Yes | Yes | Analytics |

- Data is encrypted in transit: **Yes**
- Users can request that data be deleted: **No** (the game keeps no server-side data; users can reset their Advertising ID in Android settings)
- Answer **Yes** to "Does your app use the Advertising ID?" (the AD_ID permission is declared).
